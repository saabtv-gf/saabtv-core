The wrapper is MIT licensed. The bundled mpv and FFmpeg components retain their
respective upstream licenses. Saab TV is licensed under GPL-3.0.

Local packaging modification: private FFmpeg and libc++ ELF filenames, SONAMEs,
DT_NEEDED entries, and GNU ELF version-requirement filenames are namespaced with
the libsaabmpv_ prefix. This prevents them from colliding with the application's
existing playback native libraries while remaining valid for Android's strict
dynamic linker.
The Java 21 wrapper bytecode is replaced by the same MIT-licensed wrapper built
from source by Saab TV for its Java 17 target.
